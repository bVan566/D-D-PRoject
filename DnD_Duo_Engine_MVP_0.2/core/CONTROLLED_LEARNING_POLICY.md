# Controlled Learning Policy — MVP 0.2

After each session:
1. Lore records canonical state.
2. DM reviews pacing, fairness, mechanics, player agency, and engagement.
3. Player Agent reviews independence, balance, tactical behavior, and character consistency.
4. Human player may provide feedback.
5. Observations are classified as single-session, recurring, or confirmed preference.
6. Agents may recommend behavioral improvements.
7. Changes that affect persistent guidance require human approval before becoming durable.

Learning may improve execution inside a role.
Learning may not change:
- role boundaries
- information permissions
- human-player authority
- content boundaries
- canon categories
- rules authority
- approval requirements
- governing standards
